How StarIntel works

  1. 01 / COLLECT

    Bring in documents

    Collectors turn source material into structured StarIntel documents.

  2. 02 / PROCESS

    Actors handle the work

    An actor is a service that receives messages, performs a specific task and emits results. It is not an AI agent. Some services can call AI tools.

  3. 03 / CONNECT

    Store and investigate

    Validated documents go into CouchDB. Relations connect people, organizations, findings and evidence.

  4. 04 / SHARE

    Review and publish

    Operators approve research for publication. Public pages and Radar expose only the fields permitted by their projections.
