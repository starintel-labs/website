Symbolic AI · Expert Systems · Verified Reasoning

StarIntel is a local-first, document-driven intelligence platform built the way expert systems were meant to be built: Prolog for planning and verification, actors for deterministic orchestration, structured documents with provenance for everything the system knows — and statistical models exactly where they earn their place. Not a chatbot with a database bolted on.

Prolog Actors Expert systems Structured intelligence Common Lisp Nim LLMs where appropriate

Most of the industry calls a language model the architecture. StarIntel calls it a component. Knowledge is modeled explicitly, decisions are reasoned over symbolically, and every claim that leaves the system carries provenance and evidence. When a model is the right tool — extraction, drafting, enrichment — it runs inside the same typed, verified pipelines as everything else, and its output is never the final authority.

The system, in parts

evidence-backed surfaces only
Expert systems Symbolic planning & verification On-disk Prolog knowledge bases plan work, track dependencies, and verify invariants before anything ships. Research publication is gated by machine-checked approval state, not vibes.
Actors Deterministic orchestration Sento actors and RabbitMQ topic exchanges route typed messages between services. Documents flow through documents.ingest and documents.new with recursive, supervised dataflow.
Structured intelligence StarIntel v0.9 documents Fifty typed document kinds — people, orgs, claims, relations, evidence — each carrying sources, evidence, assessment, and verification metadata. Schema-validated, hash-pinned, machine-checkable.
Autonomous research Auto Dig & Auto Research Investigator and research actors that work the corpus autonomously — and publish nothing that has not passed explicit human approval gates with recorded evidence.
Operator workspace Quasar A graph-first operator interface for investigating the corpus: typed projections, capability discovery, offline-first workspaces, and the visual DNA this site borrows.
Publishing Org-mode as the source of truth Editorial and research content is authored in Org, exported deterministically, validated by Prolog, and published with full provenance — no CMS, no markdown drift.
