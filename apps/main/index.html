symbolic systems · documents · actors · research

StarIntel is a document-driven intelligence platform. Everything the system knows is a typed, versioned StarIntel document — one of fifty document kinds in the hash-pinned v0.9 schema — validated on write, stored in CouchDB, and routed between services by Sento actors over RabbitMQ topic exchanges.

Publishing is a pipeline, not a CMS: research and editorial content is written in Org, exported deterministically, validated against the schema, checked by a Prolog verification suite, and released with recorded provenance — repository, path, commit, and content hash. Public surfaces are fail-closed projections of that pipeline.

The system, in parts

every claim maps to code
documents StarIntel v0.9 documents Fifty typed document kinds — people, orgs, claims, evidence, relations — each carrying sources, evidence, and handling metadata. Validation runs against a sha256-pinned schema; anything that fails is rejected, not coerced. implemented
actors Sento actors on RabbitMQ Supervised Sento actor systems move documents across topic exchanges (documents.ingest.#, documents.new.target.#) with durable CouchDB outbox mutation, recovery, and retry policy. implemented
publishing Approval-gated research Nothing publishes merely because it exists. Research documents must carry an explicit APPROVED state with recorded evidence before the renderer will touch them; every artifact traces back to repo, path, commit, and content hash. implemented
operator workspace Quasar A graph-first operator workspace for investigating the corpus as a graph — typed projections, relations, offline-first sessions. This site's design language is its sibling. in development
