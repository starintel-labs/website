prolog-rlm

Prolog is the control plane for StarIntel’s reasoning about itself: task knowledge bases with dependency graphs, restartable state, invariant verification suites, and the research-approval machinery (prolog-rlm.research-approval.v1) that gates what the platform is allowed to publish.

What it does

  • Planning: on-disk task KBs; ready/1, blocked/2, next_task/1 derive executable order from declared dependencies and persisted progress.
  • Verification: invariant suites that fail non-zero - no GitHub Actions in CI, public radar schemas allowlisted, published Org carrying provenance, v0.9 documents validating.
  • Approval: lifecycle status (DRAFT, RESEARCHING, REVIEW, DONE) and approval state are independent dimensions; approval requires an actor, evidence, and pinned base commit/blob.

Why Prolog

Because publication rules, invariants, and dependency ordering are logic. Encoding them in a general-purpose language invites drift; encoding them in Prolog makes the rules executable, inspectable, and falsifiable.
