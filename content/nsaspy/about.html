I build StarIntel: a local-first, document-driven intelligence platform. The thesis is simple: language models are useful components, but reasoning, planning, and verification belong to symbolic systems.

StarIntel combines:

  • Expert systems - Prolog knowledge bases that plan work and verify invariants before anything ships.
  • Actors - supervised message-passing services (Common Lisp, Sento, RabbitMQ topic exchanges) with recursive dataflow.
  • Structured documents - the StarIntel v0.9 model: fifty typed document kinds, each carrying sources, evidence, assessment, and verification metadata.
  • Autonomous workers - Auto Dig investigates; Auto Research writes; both publish nothing that has not passed explicit human approval gates.

Everything is Nix-built, schema-pinned, and provenance-tracked. When a model is the right tool - extraction, drafting, enrichment - it runs inside the same typed pipelines as everything else, and its output is never the final authority.
