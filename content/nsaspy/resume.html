Selected work

StarIntel platform (2023-present)

Architect and sole maintainer of the StarIntel stack:

  • starintel-server - Common Lisp API gateway, ingest, persistence, search, and actor routing over CouchDB, RabbitMQ, and Sento actors. Nix-built binaries, tests, and container images.
  • StarIntel document model v0.9 - fifty typed document kinds with provenance, evidence, assessment, and verification metadata; JSON Schema with expansion registries; implementations in Python, Nim, JavaScript, and Common Lisp.
  • Quasar - graph-first operator workspace (browser UI, Cytoscape rendering, offline-first workspaces).
  • Auto Dig - autonomous OSINT investigation runtime producing canonical v0.9 documents and relations at scale (Nim).
  • Auto Research - autonomous research pipeline whose outputs pass through explicit human approval gates with recorded evidence.
  • prolog-rlm - Prolog research-approval and reasoning machinery used to gate publication and verify invariants.
  • star-cl - the StarIntel document contract ported to Common Lisp.

Infrastructure and delivery

  • Nix flakes for reproducible builds across the stack; container image pipelines; a deterministic GitHub/Forgejo synchronization service in Go.
  • Self-hosted Forgejo Actions CI for the platform’s changed-document validation and build pipelines.

Principles

  • Provenance first: every fact carries its source, evidence, and retrieval date.
  • Fail closed: unknown states are not published.
  • Symbolic control, statistical assistance: models propose, logic disposes.
  • Reproducibility: Nix, pinned schemas, deterministic exports.
